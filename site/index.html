Read the full operator brief

Take command of Nova Station, a complete retro-futuristic pinball table built for fast reactions and long score-chasing sessions.

Every launch begins with three balls and a clear objective: keep the station online. Chain bumper hits, climb the ramps, open the portal, complete timed missions, earn nine clearance promotions and trigger multiball. A precise deterministic simulation keeps every rebound, flipper strike, nudge and tilt consistent.

BUILT FOR PINBALL; Responsive two-thumb flippers; Pull-and-release plunger; Short horizontal nudge gesture with tilt protection; Interactive guide for controls, mission lanes and progression targets; Missions, score multipliers, bonuses, extra balls and multiball; Local high scores saved on your device; Optional Game Center leaderboard

ONE COMPLETE TABLE; The full 4:3 table and its status console remain visible in landscape on both iPhone and iPad. Original raster artwork, sound and haptics create a tactile premium retro-PC atmosphere without copying another game’s identity.

PLAY ON YOUR TERMS; Nova Station Pinball works offline and requires no account. It contains no advertising and no tracking. In version 1.0, the entire game is available free upfront. Optional tips in this version support development but unlock no features and do not change gameplay.

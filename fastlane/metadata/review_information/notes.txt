Nova Station Pinball is an original landscape pinball game. Use the on-screen flipper controls and plunger to play; the table guide is available from the home screen.

For submitted version 1.0, the current release configuration offers the complete game free upfront and it is playable without an account. Game Center is optional and is used only for leaderboards. Cancelling Game Center authentication does not block local play or local scores.

For this submitted version, Settings offers the three optional consumable tips declared by the release configuration: Coffee, Big thanks and Strong support. They unlock no feature, content, entitlement or service. This version has no advertising, analytics, tracking, paywall or subscription. No demo credentials are required.

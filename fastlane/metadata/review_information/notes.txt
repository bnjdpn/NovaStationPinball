Nova Station Pinball is an original landscape pinball game. Use the on-screen flipper controls and plunger to play; the table guide is available from the home screen.

The complete game is free and playable without an account. Game Center is optional and is used only for leaderboards. Cancelling Game Center authentication does not block local play or local scores.

Settings offers exactly three optional consumable tips: Coffee, Big thanks and Strong support. They unlock no feature, content, entitlement or service. There is no advertising, analytics, tracking, paywall or subscription. No demo credentials are required.

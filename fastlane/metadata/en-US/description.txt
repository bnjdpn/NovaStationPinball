THE WORKSHOP
Because the simulation is strictly deterministic, Nova Station can put a lost ball back exactly where it was. The Workshop lifts the rewind limit: go back three seconds, five seconds or to the start of the ball as often as you want, with the table handed straight back to you at the instant you choose. It adds a replay that starts five seconds back instead of replaying the whole ball, and 14 shot drills that serve the ball from an identical position as many times as you like — each one keeping its own success rate and best streak on your device.

HONEST SCORES
Rewinding is assistance, so it is treated as such. Any game in which you rewind or replay a ball — the three free rewinds included — is ranked on a separate training board and is never sent to the Game Center leaderboard. Scores you have already set stay exactly as they are.
